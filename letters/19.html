Dear Dr. Georgiev, Dr. Nikolov and the Sofiamed team,

I am writing to supplement my inquiry of 16 November and my follow-up of 18 November, to give you a fuller picture of my current condition before any review of my case.

For the third year I have been practically unable to work. I spend most of my time at rest and do only light household work. After about half an hour of ordinary work I must lie down for about two days; after two or three hours of activity, for about a week. Even after one hour of sitting on a chair at a table I need to lie down until the pressing pain subsides. I also tolerate standing poorly: even without physical exertion I feel ill when standing.

Regarding my left testicle: I have constant discomfort in it. During my third varicocele operation I was warned that the extensive removal of veins in the previous operations limited the possibility of adequately restoring the blood circulation of the left testicle.

Why documenting my condition matters to me: I have never served in the army, and my documents record that I am unfit for military service in peacetime because of this disease. I am currently afraid to leave home because of the fear of forced mobilization without prior stenting to restore blood flow. This fear also prevents me from seeking medical care in person, so I ask you, if possible, to document my functional limitations and medical risks in your assessment.

My concern is not only pain but the possibility of irreversible damage to the veins that remain. I would like to ask whether delaying treatment, in particular if I were forcibly mobilized and unable to avoid actions that worsen my condition, could create a risk of irreversible damage to venous circulation or tissue function. I would also be grateful for guidance on which activities and loads are dangerous for me and which restrictions should be documented.

As before, I ask to begin with a remote review. I am seeking an assessment and an explanation of options, not giving consent to an operation. If any review, services or materials are chargeable, please inform me of this and of the approximate cost in advance, as I do not currently consent to any paid services.


Thank you for your time.
