VitaMed, diagnostic center, Odesa, 5 Akademika Vorobiova St., building 11. Computed tomograph Philips Brilliance 64, magnetic resonance tomograph Philips Achieva 1.5 T.

MAGNETIC RESONANCE IMAGING OF THE LUMBAR SPINE. Mikhaliov A.V., born 04.12.1972. Date of examination: 03.04.2024.

At the time of the MR examination the lumbar spine has a flattened lordosis; the spinal axis is not curved.

The vertebral bodies have the usual configuration. The bone marrow in the vertebral bodies has a heterogeneous MR signal intensity due to areas of fatty degeneration. Hemangiomas up to 25 mm in diameter are seen in the bodies of the L1-L5 vertebrae.

Sagittal size of the spinal canal at the L3 level: 15 mm. The conus medullaris is at the L1 level and divides into the root filaments of the cauda equina.

L1-L2: dorsal disc bulge up to 2 mm. L2-L3: dorsal disc bulge up to 2 mm. L3-L4: dorsal disc bulge up to 2 mm. L4-L5: dorsal disc bulge up to 2 mm. L5-S1: dorsal median protrusion of the intervertebral disc up to 3.5 mm, moderately deforming the dural sac.

Schmorl's nodes are seen along the endplates of the L1-S1 vertebral bodies. Bony outgrowths of the anterior edges of the L1-S1 vertebral bodies are seen.

The facet joints L1-S1 are hypertrophied and deformed with marginal bony outgrowths, which cause moderate narrowing of the bony root canals. No pathological changes in the soft tissues at the level of the examination.

Conclusion: Osteochondrosis of the lumbar spine. Protrusion of the L5-S1 intervertebral disc. Schmorl's nodes of the L1-S1 vertebral bodies. Spondyloarthrosis. Hemangiomas of the L1-L5 vertebral bodies.

Doctor: Korotynska Ye.A. (signature, stamp). Note at the bottom of the form: for a repeat examination, bring the images (disc, film) and the protocols of previous examinations.
