What I am asking

I am looking for a healthcare institution that will either take me on for treatment or write a petition for referral abroad under Resolution No. 1079 of the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine. A reply to any one of the points below is enough.

The case in a few lines

  • May-Thurner syndrome, compression of the left iliac vein, I87.1, CEAP C4. Diagnosis confirmed by MRI with contrast, vascular surgeon's entry of 2026 (eHealth treatment plan No. 7530-0736-7274-7824), stenting recommended and planned.
  • Second level: iatrogenic pseudo-pincer of the iliac vein at the scar after the 1997 Ivanissevich operation, described after venography by a urologist, a professor. I need both levels measured before the length and position of the stent are chosen.
  • According to the reply of the National Academy of Medical Sciences, recommendations and consensus documents (American Venous Forum, Society for Vascular Surgery) provide for endovascular treatment of May-Thurner syndrome with dedicated venous stents and intravascular imaging (the reply is in the No. 1079 section).
  • Chronic pain in the left leg and in the left pelvis; walking about 2 km or carrying a bucket of water makes it worse. I cannot work at the moment.
  • Available: pelvic MRI in DICOM format, doctors' conclusions, discharge summaries, an eHealth extract, ultrasound. No new CT, MR venography or venography yet.

What exactly I ask of a clinic

  • Review my materials and say whether my case is suitable for IVUS-guided stenting, and which tests are needed before the procedure.
  • Say whether IVUS will be used throughout the procedure (choosing diameter and length, positioning the stent, final assessment), and what the clinic thinks about the second (distal) level.
  • If the clinic is in Ukraine: write a petition to the Health Department of the Odesa Regional Military Administration under No. 1079. The documents the department asks for are described in the No. 1079 section (patient's application with consent to data processing, extract from the medical record stating that the care is not possible in Ukraine, consent to transfer data to a foreign clinic, undertaking to submit a report after treatment).
  • If the clinic is abroad: state in writing whether it is ready to accept me for treatment, the preliminary treatment plan and the approximate cost. Such a letter is needed for the No. 1079 documents. If the clinic already has experience with No. 1079 or with patients from Ukraine, I would be grateful for advice on how to arrange it.
  • Record in the conclusion the functional limitations the doctor sees, within the doctor's clinical assessment.

Why treatment abroad is hard to arrange

During martial law, men aged 23 to 60 who are Ukrainian citizens are restricted from leaving the country. The Rules for crossing the state border (Cabinet Resolution of 1995 No. 57, para. 2-1) list exceptions, including persons with a disability who hold the confirming certificate. I am 53 and have no disability status, so I do not have this ground now. My doctor (family doctor) agrees to refer me to ECOPFO (the expert team for assessing a person's daily functioning, through which disability is established) only after stenting is scheduled. That is why a petition under No. 1079 or a written confirmation that treatment is scheduled matters to me.

Payment and cost

Please state the approximate cost of treatment (an estimate: consultation, tests, IVUS and stents, hospital stay, follow-up, possible extra costs). The estimate is for documents and planning. It is not consent to paid services, and I promise nothing in advance. Please discuss payment separately and in writing, before any paid service. Funding under No. 1079 is decided by the Ministry of Health of Ukraine after a commission decision; there is no such decision today.

Invitation to a vascular surgeon

If you are a vascular surgeon, especially in Odesa or the Odesa region, and are ready to become my lead doctor, please write or call. A lead doctor can review the materials, order the missing tests, arrange stenting under IVUS or write a petition under No. 1079. I ask to begin with telemedicine: a remote consultation and remote follow-up. An in-person visit comes later, if the doctor considers it necessary. This is an invitation, not a demand.
