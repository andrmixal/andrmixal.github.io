Resolution No. 1079 and the unavailability of treatment

This section is compiled only from the letters and organization replies on this site (section "Letters", replies from organizations). It is a retelling of the documents, without judgments. Numbers and dates are as in the documents. Each reply can be opened in full (links at the bottom).

1. What is needed

  • Stenting of the left iliac vein under intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) guidance for May-Thurner syndrome (ICD-10 I87.1). Diagnosis and plan: entry in the eHealth system of 28 Apr 2026, planned stenting, treatment plan No. 7530-0736-7274-7824.
  • A doctor or center is sought where venous IVUS exists and is used in practice, or a referral for treatment abroad under Resolution No. 1079.

2. What Resolution No. 1079 is (according to the replies of the department and the Ministry of Health)

  • Cabinet of Ministers Resolution of 27 Dec 2017 No. 1079 "On ensuring the organization of referral of citizens of Ukraine for treatment abroad". Under paragraph 1, citizens are referred on condition that such care is not possible in Ukrainian institutions. This is confirmed by a conclusion of the Ministry of Health expert group of the relevant profile (Ministry of Health, 04.3/58467/2-26; Health Department of the Odesa Regional Military Administration, 9158).
  • The referral is initiated by a highly specialized multi-profile or single-profile healthcare institution: it writes a petition in free form and sends it to the regional health department (Department, 9158).
  • Within 10 working days the department prepares and submits to the Ministry of Health in electronic form: a referral letter with a list of documents (with the qualified electronic signature of the head), the citizen's application with consent to processing of medical data (annex 1), an extract from the medical record stating that care is not possible in Ukrainian institutions, consent to transfer data to foreign institutions and an undertaking to submit a report after treatment (annex 2) (Department, 9158).
  • The final assessment of whether such care (in his case with IVUS and dedicated venous stents) is available nationwide is made by the Ministry of Health profile expert group and the Ministry of Health Commission on referral of citizens for treatment abroad (Ministry of Health, 04.3/58467/2-26).

3. What was asked and who replied what

  • National Health Service of Ukraine (NHSU), request of 23 Jun 2026: whether IVUS can be used in planned interventions for May-Thurner syndrome under the medical guarantees program. Reply No. 17512/8-11-26 of 27 Jul 2026: there is no separate "Vascular surgery" package in 2026; planned vascular operations, including stenting of iliac veins, are paid under the package "Surgical operations for adults and children in inpatient settings". Mandatory availability and use of IVUS is not set by the specifications and conditions for purchasing services for 2026. NHSU considers it appropriate to work out the question of mandatory IVUS at the Ministry of Health level, as the body that forms sector standards, and promises to take the information into account when preparing proposals for specifications for later periods.
  • NHSU, requests of 28 Jul 2026 and 14 Aug 2026: coverage of the cost of a venous stent and consumables, training of specialists in the IVUS method. Reply No. 23740/8-11-26 of 13 Oct 2026: there is no separate tariff for the cost of a stent for this package; patient co-payment is not provided; service providers are prohibited from demanding payment from patients for services under the program; there is no mechanism by which a patient pays for a device for a service under the program; charitable aid is regulated by a separate law and is outside NHSU competence; NHSU does not run programs paying for venous stents above the NHSU tariff in 2026; centralized purchase of medical devices is within the competence of the Ministry of Health.
  • NHSU, requests of 8-9 Jan 2026: reply No. 4531/8-11-26 of 11 Feb 2026: the package "Surgical operations" includes endovascular interventions; stenting of venous vessels is paid under this package if the institution has a contract with NHSU. The cost of devices purchased centrally from the state budget is not part of the tariffs (paragraph 7 of the Procedure). NHSU assigns to the Ministry of Health the questions of including venous stents in the list of centralized purchases, clinical protocols, adaptation of international recommendations (ESVS 2022) and indications for preventive stenting at the pre-thrombotic stage.
  • Ministry of Health, reply to a public information request of 19 Jan 2026 (04.4/3374/2-26): at present the list of devices purchased from the state budget (Cabinet Resolution of 7 Mar 2022 No. 216) does not include medical devices for treating patients with venous disease of the iliac veins.
  • National Academy of Medical Sciences (NAMS), No. 3-03/1719 (23 Jul 2026, reply to the secretariat of the Ombudsman): existing recommendations and consensus documents (American Venous Forum, Society for Vascular Surgery) provide for endovascular technologies with dedicated venous stents and intravascular imaging in the treatment of May-Thurner syndrome. Regulations on equipment of institutions (vascular and endovascular surgery departments) do not require IVUS equipment. The devices for treatment are expensive and are not in the list purchased from the state budget under Resolution No. 216. NAMS has repeatedly informed the Ministry of Health and NHSU of the need to widen this list.
  • Ministry of Health, 04.5/39243/2-26 (17 Jul 2026): the Ministry asks NAMS and the Health Department of the Odesa Regional Military Administration, within their competence, to consider the questions and to provide A. Mikhalov, on medical indications, with the necessary care at budget expense within available funding; it describes the referral procedure abroad under No. 1079 and under Ministry order No. 574 (for the period of martial law).
  • Ministry of Health, 04.3/58467/2-26 (reply to requests of 26 Aug 2026 and 12 Sep 2026 on applying No. 1079): individual institutions (including the Shalimov Center) assess care within their statutory powers and resources; institutions are not authorized to monitor nationwide the availability of equipment or consumables in all other hospitals, so their replies concern only the capacity of the institution itself. The Ministry also describes the evacuation mechanism to EU countries under order No. 574 and EU temporary protection (treatment is financed by the host country). For a decision on referral the Ministry advises contacting a doctor of the institution that coordinates referral.
  • Health Department of the Odesa Regional Military Administration: 5480 (17 Jun 2026, to the request of 16 Jun 2026) describes the medical evacuation mechanism (Ministry of Health and European Commission) and the categories of patients; 9158 (25 Aug 2026, to the request of 7 Aug 2026) repeats the No. 1079 procedure and names letter No. 6279 of 14 Jul 2026 as explanations given earlier; 9891 (3 Nov 2026, to the request of 16 Oct 2026): to resolve the matter, the list of documents under No. 1079 must be submitted to the Department.
  • NHSU No. 4220/13-11-26 (9 Feb 2026): NHSU sent a request to the provider (the Shalimov Center) asking for explanations on the appeal; it promised to report the result separately. NAMS No. 5-03/1071: remote appeal of 8 Jan 2026; at that time the institution had no NHSU contract for the outpatient services package; for the period of review it began contracting; no repeat appeal on the e-referral was recorded.
  • NHSU No. 5965/24-12-26 (2 Mar 2026): on entering medical information from doctors abroad into the eHealth system: the patient can contact a doctor (family doctor or specialist) of an institution with an NHSU contract with a written conclusion; after clinical assessment the doctor may enter records into the system.
  • Anadolu Medical Center (Turkey), 11 Aug 2026: from the documents sent, the changes are compatible with May-Thurner syndrome; endovascular stenting of the iliac vein is possible, under IVUS if needed; invitation for further tests and treatment. Preliminary cost: consultation 160 USD; stenting under IVUS 10,500 EUR (plus or minus 10%), payment before each procedure.

4. Where replies differ or end with a referral to another body

  • Two mechanisms: the replies of the Ministry and the department describe both No. 1079 and Ministry order No. 574 (evacuation for the period of martial law). For No. 1079 the department names a petition from a specialized institution and documents submitted to the department; for No. 574 the Ministry names a doctor of the coordinating institution who fills in form 027/o.
  • That treatment is not possible in Ukraine under No. 1079 is confirmed by the Ministry's expert group. At the same time the Ministry writes that institutions answer only about their own capacity and do not monitor equipment nationwide. NAMS and NHSU write that there is no mandatory IVUS equipment and that the specifications do not require it.
  • NHSU: providers must ensure the equipment, devices and consumables needed for paid services (paragraph 10 of the Procedure); there is no separate tariff for a stent; co-payment and payment for a device by the patient are not provided; centrally purchased devices are not part of the tariff. Ministry of Health and NAMS: devices for iliac veins are not in the list of centralized purchases.
  • Each body assigns the questions of IVUS, clinical protocols and the purchase list to another: NHSU to the Ministry, NAMS to the Ministry and NHSU, the department to the Ministry and to the institution that must write the petition.
