I only recently learned that I can be cured completely, as an outpatient, in about half an hour. In Ukraine, nitinol stents are not placed correctly to restore blood flow. Both compressions, the congenital one and the iatrogenic one, are treated with one stent.

Summary

Born 1972, Odesa region, Ukraine. Non-thrombotic May-Thurner syndrome (NIVL), compression of the left iliac vein, ICD-10 I87.1, CEAP C4. Chronic left leg venous pain and chronic pelvic pain.

Function: after about 30 minutes of work I have to lie down for about 2 days; after 2 to 3 hours, for about a week. Details: Symptoms and complaints.

Since my youth I have been unable to work for long or much; this limit is now at its strongest. Because of pain I have been forced to abstain from intimacy for years. All my life I have dreamed of a family and children, and treatment is my chance for that. For example, at the International Rescue Committee, the most interesting English-language international job for me, I was asked to vacate the drivers' rest room for meetings. I wrote to my manager that, because of my venous disease, I do not have the strength to stand on my feet for long in the corridor waiting for the meeting to end. I lost this job because of my venous disease and my inability to stand for long. Otherwise this job suited me: I can drive, communicate in English and learn all the rules without any problem. I left my last job at a ship chandler company because of a flare-up of this disease, although I had the prospect of becoming deputy to the company's founder in all areas and was already effectively working in the office. My knees also began to hurt then, but I was invited to work in the office, and there my knees did not get in the way; venous stasis did not let me work at the computer.

Diagnosis confirmed instrumentally (MRI with contrast) and recorded by a vascular surgeon on 2026. Stenting of the left iliac vein is recommended and planned. Stenting and hospitalization have not yet been done, for reasons not related to medicine. (vascular surgeon's record, transcription and translation) National health system (eHealth) treatment plan No. 7530-0736-7274-7824 of 2026; e-referral No. 1733-4270-3364-4529 of 2026 for the vascular surgeon consultation.

Requirement: stenting under IVUS guidance, by a surgeon with practical venous IVUS experience. By my requests (Feofania clinic, Odesa National Medical University, NAMS, NHSU, Ministry of Health, 2026) no center in Ukraine has confirmed that it performs iliac vein stenting under IVUS. The replies are collected in Letters and Resolution 1079.

Second finding: iatrogenic pseudo-pincer of the iliac vein after the 1997 Ivanissevich operation, described by a urologist, a professor. It lies at the level of the scar, below the May-Thurner compression. IVUS is needed to measure both.

What I am asking

I ask for a clinic that will take me on for treatment, or write a petition for treatment abroad under Resolution No. 1079. Details: What I am asking.

Second issue: nose and sinuses

Nose: CT of the paranasal sinuses of 2024 shows signs of chronic maxillary sinusitis of the left maxillary sinus. ENT consultation of 2024: chronic left maxillary sinusitis, recommended: endoscopic left maxillotomy (revision), planned. Not done, for reasons not related to medicine. Details and documents: Nose and sinuses.
