Symptoms

  • Venous claudication: a bursting sensation builds while walking.
  • Cannot tolerate prolonged standing, sitting or travel. Pain builds up and subsides slowly, up to a week.
  • After about 30 minutes of work he must lie down for about 2 days; after 2 to 3 hours, about a week. Even an hour at a desk requires lying down until the pressure-like pain subsides.
  • Chronic pulling and stabbing pain in the left lower abdomen, deep under the 1997 scar, radiating to the lower back.
  • Palpation of the left inguinal and scrotal area causes strong pain that lasts days.
  • Pain is worse in the cold and at night, from immobility. Compression garments are contraindicated.
  • Walking about 2 km or carrying a bucket of water increases pain; the right knee gives way.
  • The left knee gives way after prolonged walking.
  • Prolonged trips are not tolerated: circulation does not compensate.
