Symptoms and complaints

Work capacity

  • After about 30 minutes of work I have to lie down for about 2 days. After 2 to 3 hours of work I have to lie down for about a week.
  • Even an hour at a desk makes me lie down until the pressure-like pain passes.
  • I cannot tolerate prolonged standing, sitting or travel. The pain builds up and subsides slowly, over up to a week.

Left leg and pelvis

  • Chronic pulling and stabbing pain in the lower left abdomen, deep under the 1997 scar, radiating to the lower back.
  • The pain moves: it can be in the lower abdomen on the left, the leg, the buttock, the groin or the lower back on the left.
  • In the left leg a bursting sensation builds while walking.
  • Palpation of the left inguinal and scrotal area causes strong pain that lasts for days.
  • The pain is worse in the cold and at night. It increases markedly when I stand, sit or stay still for a long time.

Walking and knees

  • Walking causes a pronounced limitation of support and movement, with limping.
  • Walking about 2 km or carrying a bucket of water increases the pain.
  • Since the arthroscopy of the right knee in 2024, my right knee gives way after one or two kilometers.
  • At the Odrex orthopedic consultation in 2024, a combined contracture of the right knee joint was recorded (extension 175, flexion 130), with a recommendation to limit physical load on the right knee.
  • The bursting sensation in the left leg and the giving way of the right knee are separate complaints.

Other

  • Nose: paresthesia (itching) on the left for two years, constant heaviness; at night and in the cold the nose does not breathe on the left. Details: Nose and sinuses.
  • Eyes: pain in the right eye. In the dark I have pronounced photopsias, bright flashes of light clearly synchronized with the movements of the eyeball. History: myopia of medium degree was corrected with LASIK in 1999. Preventive laser coagulation of the retina of both eyes was done in 1999. At the 2002 check-up visual acuity was 1.0 in both eyes (conclusion of the Tarus laser vision correction center at the Filatov Institute, Odesa).

Life and work

Since my youth I have been unable to work for long or much; this limit is now at its strongest. Because of pain I have been forced to abstain from intimacy for years. All my life I have dreamed of a family and children, and treatment is my chance for that. For example, at the International Rescue Committee, the most interesting English-language international job for me, I was asked to vacate the drivers' rest room for meetings. I wrote to my manager that, because of my venous disease, I do not have the strength to stand on my feet for long in the corridor waiting for the meeting to end. I lost this job because of my venous disease and my inability to stand for long. Otherwise this job suited me: I can drive, communicate in English and learn all the rules without any problem. I left my last job at a ship chandler company because of a flare-up of this disease, although I had the prospect of becoming deputy to the company's founder in all areas and was already effectively working in the office. My knees also began to hurt then, but I was invited to work in the office, and there my knees did not get in the way; venous stasis did not let me work at the computer.
