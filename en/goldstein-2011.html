Discharge summary (epicrisis) No. 5800. Mikhalev A.V., born 1972. Address: Ak. Hlushko 3/12. Urology department No. 1, City Clinical Hospital No. 10. From 29.06.11 to 06.07.11.

Complaints: constant pulling pain in the left half of the scrotum, increasing with the slightest physical exertion, by the end of the working day.

History: left-sided varicocele was diagnosed in 1992. In 1997 he underwent the Ivanissevich operation on the left. Because the pain syndrome returned, in 1999: excision of varicose veins of the left spermatic cord. Admitted as planned for surgical treatment.

Examination. Blood group: O (I) Rh (-) negative.

ECG: sinus arrhythmia, heart rate 67-70 beats per minute. Compared with the ECG of 22.04.11 and the present one, the changes are less pronounced, i.e. positive dynamics.

CBC /27.06.11/: Hb 146, RBC 4.5, color index 0.97, WBC 6.0, ESR 4, eos 1, band 5, seg 65, lymph 26, mono 3, glucose 4.3. Blood chemistry /27.06.11/: PTI 89%, fibrinogen 2.44, ethanol test negative, total bilirubin 14.69, thymol test 1.7[unclear], AST 0.31, ALT 0.43, amylase 16, urea 5.3, creatinine 88, total protein 65. Urinalysis /27.06.11/: straw yellow, clear, specific gravity 1013, acidic, protein none, WBC 0-1 per field, RBC unchanged, single per field.

Ultrasound: copies handed over.

DIAGNOSIS: Recurrent left-sided varicocele. Orchialgia.

Considering the persistent pain syndrome in the left half of the scrotum, the ineffectiveness of long conservative therapy, and the patient's firm insistence on getting rid of the pain: 30.06.11, operation by Goldstein on the left.

In the postoperative period he received ceftriaxone, Detralex, dressings. The wound heals by primary intention. The veins of the left spermatic cord decreased in volume. No allergic reactions were observed during the stay. No sick leave needed.

RECOMMENDED: treatment by the polyclinic urologist at the place of residence. 1. Dressings. 2. Remove stitches on 08.07.11. 3. Limit physical load for 1 month. 4. Suspensory for 10 days. 5. Detralex 500 mg twice a day for 10 days. 6. Follow-up in the andrology department of City Clinical Hospital No. 10.

Head of department: V.M. Khodos. Attending physician: A.V. Riasnyi. Stamp: Health Administration of Odesa City Council, Municipal Institution "City Clinical Hospital No. 10", code 01999052; stamp "Chancellery".
